Why this page is kept separate

It is measured in points, not percent. These are spreads, so the number moves and not just the price. Comparing a price taken at +2.5 against a price taken at −1.5 would be comparing two different bets. Points of line movement is the honest unit here, and it is why the NBA figure is never averaged into the site-wide CLV.

The sample is eight days old. 65 picks. Whatever this page says, it does not yet say it with any confidence, and a run of good numbers over a week is exactly what noise looks like.

The entry gate behind these picks is unproven and cannot be reviewed yet. It was written before any NBA data existed and is frozen under a pre-registration until 1 December 2026. Nothing on this page may be used to change it early — reading a result and then adjusting the rule that produced it is how a book starts fooling itself.

The log records one pick per game, not all of them. The screener runs several times a day, and only the first qualifying run per game is written here. A later run that picked the other side is invisible on this page. That is a known defect in the log, registered rather than patched over.
