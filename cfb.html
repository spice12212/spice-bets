Why this board is in points

These are spreads, so the number moves, not just the price. Taking −9.5 and watching it close at −10.5 is a full point of value that a price-only percentage scores as nothing. The line moved on 55% of the 359 picks here with both numbers, so points is the honest unit and this figure is never averaged into the site-wide CLV. The price comparison is kept for the 157 picks whose line held.

This changed on 6 November 2026 and it made the site-wide figure look better. Before that this board was pooled into the headline percentage. Named here rather than quietly swapped.
