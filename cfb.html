Why this board is in points

These are spreads, so the number moves and not just the price. Taking −9.5 and watching it close at −10.5 is a full point of value, and a percentage built from prices alone scores it as nothing at all. Points of line movement is the honest unit, which is why this figure is never averaged into the site-wide CLV.

It is not a rare problem on this board. The line moved on 56% of the 240 picks here that have both an entry and a closing number. The price comparison is still shown, restricted to the 102 picks whose line never moved, where it does mean something.

This changed on 6 November 2026, and it made the site-wide figure look better. Before that, this board was pooled into the headline percentage. It is named here and on the home page rather than quietly swapped.
